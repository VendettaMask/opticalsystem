This directory contains project-authored code, validation outputs and source
snapshots. Measured source material is read from the earlier frozen Cornell and
VIAVI evidence packages; their original provenance/third-party notices apply.
No claim of ownership or new independent instrument measurement is made.

Cornell official data entry: https://bowers.cornell.edu/computer-graphics/data
VIAVI official entry: https://www.viavisolutions.com/en-us/osp/resources/bsdf-data
Official CIE observer and illuminant table identities remain in xyz-conditions
and xyz-radiometry. The original published Cornell XYZ observer is unverified.

The new Grid/9 CI fixture contains synthetic analytical data only. RBF local
systems and log angular evaluation are project-authored pure C#; independent
NumPy/SciPy experiments do not enter product runtime or supply runtime results.
