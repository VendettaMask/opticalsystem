SiO2 and Ta2O5 thin-film optical constants

Original YAML files are preserved beside this notice.

L. V. Rodriguez-de Marcos, J. I. Larruquert, J. A. Mendez, J. A. Aznarez.
Self-consistent optical constants of SiO2 and Ta2O5 films.
Opt. Mater. Express 6, 3622-3637 (2016), https://doi.org/10.1364/OME.6.003622
Reactive electron-beam evaporation, 300 degrees C.

Both n and k are retained. Wavelengths alone are converted from micrometers to nanometers.
These datasets describe the cited samples, not universal deposition-process constants.

Additional measured-film validation tables (2026-11-01)
Same fixed database commit and CC0 dedication as above. Original tabulated n and k
are preserved without removing absorption or extrapolating the wavelength range.

TiO2-Siefke.yml: 350 nm ALD film; ellipsometry/spectrophotometry and universal
dispersion fit. T. Siefke et al., Adv. Opt. Mater. 4, 1780-1786 (2016),
https://doi.org/10.1002/adom.201600250

Nb2O5-Lemarchand.yml: F. Lemarchand private communication (2013), 500 nm
magnetron-sputtered monolayer on BK7; measurement method:
https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.20.015734

HfO2-Siefke.yml: 220 nm PEALD film on SiO2, T. Siefke et al. (2026),
https://doi.org/10.1051/jeos/2026036
Its table ends at 619.921 nm; only covered specifications are used.
These tables describe the measured samples and their reported fits, not the
optical constants of every manufacturing process or production certification.
