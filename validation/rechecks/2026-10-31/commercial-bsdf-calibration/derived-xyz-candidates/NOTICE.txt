These measured originals are validation inputs, not a shipped material library or test fixture.

krylon-blue-derived-xyz.csv is a modified/derived diagnostic dataset, not an independent XYZ measurement.
Derivation: linearly interpolate original 10 nm BRDF to 1 nm, multiply the original CIE CMFs,
trapezoid integrate a specified equal-energy test illuminant limited to 400-700 nm, normalize
to unit Y of an ideal white reflector within that band; no D65 assumption or spectrum extrapolation.
Attribution and applicable ShareAlike terms for CIE-derived data are preserved here.
